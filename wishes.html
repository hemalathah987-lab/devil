My Dear Chellemma 🥰❤️

Happy Birthday to my dear devil! ❤️❤️

First of all, I just want to say that I’m so lucky to have you as my sister. Nuvvu eppudu happy ga undali, life lo nuvvu anukunna prati goal reach avvali ani heartful ga korukuntunna. ❤️ I will always be there for you, no matter what happens or where life takes us.

Nuvvu eppudu ilage strong ga, bold ga, confident ga undali. Life lo enni problems vachina, nuvvu avi face chese strength ni eppudu lose avvakudadhu. Sometimes we may fight, irritate each other, or not talk properly, but whatever happens, mana bond eppatiki break avvakudadhu. 🥹❤️

You are not just my sister… you are my little princess, my partner in crime, my best friend, and one of the most important people in my life. 🫂❤️ Nuvvu naatho unna memories anni naaku chala special. Chinnapati nundi manam hostel lo kalisi gadipina days, mana silly fights, mana endless talks, navvukunna moments… ippudu avi gurthosthe chala happy ga anipisthundhi. Aa rojulu malli raavu, but those memories will always stay with us. 🥹

I’m missing you a lot! ❤️ Literally waiting to spend some quality time with you. Manam kalisi inkaa chala trips ki vellali, chala places explore cheyyali, random plans eskovali, midnight talks cheyyali, chala memories create cheyyali. 😍✈️❤️ Life busy ayina, responsibilities perigina, mana kosam konchem time eppudu save cheskundham.

And now, I feel like it’s our turn to take care of our parents. 🥹❤️ Chinnapati nundi vallu manalni entho prema tho, garabham ga pencharu. Mana kosam chala sacrifices chesaru. Ippudu manam independent ga nilabadali, life lo settle avvali, and vallani proud ga feel ayyela choodali. It's our time to give them the happiness they deserve. ❤️

Nuvvu life lo entha high ki vellina, eppudu nee roots ni, mana family ni, mana bond ni marchipovaddu. And whenever you feel low, remember that nee pakkana nenu eppudu untanu. Distance unna, situations ela unna, you can always come to me, talk to me, cry with me, laugh with me… anything. 🫂❤️

And one dream I really want to see come true is…

CHALLAGUNDLA SNEHALATHA, CA ❤️✨

Aa name ni chusi nenu, mana parents, mana family andaroo proud ga feel avvali. Nuvvu entha kashtapadalsi vachina, never give up on your dreams. I truly believe you can do it. One day, I want to proudly say, “That’s my sister!” 🥹❤️

Mana life lo enni changes vachina, manam entha busy ayina, entha dooram unna, childhood lo unna aa same sister bond eppatiki alane undali. We should always have each other's back. 🤞❤️

Once again, Happiest Birthday to my dear Chellemma! 🎂🥰❤️

May this year bring you lots of happiness, success, peace, good health and everything you wish for. Keep smiling, keep shining, keep being the crazy little devil you are! 😂❤️

Love you forever, my little princess. 🫂❤️

Your Akka ❤️
